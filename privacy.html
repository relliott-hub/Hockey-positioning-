🏒 Hockey IQ Trainer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

The short version: Hockey IQ Trainer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. Everything the app remembers stays on your own device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, or transmit personal information of any kind — no names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

Information stored on your device

So the game can remember your progress between sessions, the following is saved locally in your browser or app storage and never leaves your device:

  • The display name, jersey number, and team colour you choose in the app
  • Your experience points, level, and earned trophies
  • Your game settings (age group, difficulty, position, and format)

A display name is optional and is entirely your choice — the game works without one. This information is not transmitted anywhere, is not accessible to us, and is deleted if you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for young hockey players and the adults who coach them. Because the app collects no personal information and transmits nothing off the device, there is no personal information from children — or anyone else — for us to receive, store, use, or disclose.

Third parties, advertising, and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking software, no social media integrations, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing in the app profiles you or builds an audience segment from your use of it.

Network access

The app is designed to run entirely offline. After it first loads, it functions with no internet connection at all and makes no requests to any server we or anyone else controls.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised "last updated" date. If a future version of the app ever collects information, this policy will be updated before that version ships.
